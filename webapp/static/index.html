          📊 Model Architecture, Validation Benchmarks & Honest Known Limitations
          ▼

          Architecture & Dataset

          • Input Processing: MediaPipe Face Detector (Tasks Vision `blaze_face_short_range.tflite`) & 478-landmark landmarker (`face_landmarker.task`) extracting 5 standardized anatomical regions (Forehead, Nose, Left Cheek, Right Cheek, Chin at 224x224). Macro close-ups tiled into 1–9 patches.
          • Dual Backbone: ResNet-50 (23.52M parameters, checkpoint `resnet50_baseline_best.pt`) + EfficientNet-B0 (4.01M parameters, checkpoint `efficientnet_b0_baseline_best.pt`).
          • Validation Temperature Scaling: Fitted strictly on 294 validation images with zero test leakage: $T_{\text{resnet}} = 0.60, T_{\text{effnet}} = 1.20, w_{\text{resnet}} = 0.40, w_{\text{effnet}} = 0.60$.
          • Dataset Size & Split: 2,214 curated smartphone images across 1,491 subjects using a 70/15/15 subject-grouped stratified split (1,617 Train / 294 Val / 303 In-Domain Test), plus 337 held-out Google SCIN external test images.

          In-Domain Test Performance (303 Images)

          • Overall Accuracy: 92.74% (281 / 303 correct)
          • Macro-F1 Score: 0.8736 (improved from baseline 0.8621 via calibration)
          • Expected Calibration Error (ECE): 0.0197 (-36.0% reduction from 0.0308)
          • Per-Class F1 Breakdown:
            • Normal: 0.9804 (75 / 76 recall)
            • Acne: 0.9412 – 0.9464 (104 / 113 recall)
            • Rosacea: 0.9091 – 0.9100 (90 / 99 recall)
            • Dermatitis: 0.6207 – 0.7059 (12 / 15 recall)
          • Selective Abstention Safety: Withholding low-confidence predictions achieves 97.07% Accuracy at 90% coverage (273/303) and 99.17% Accuracy (240/242) at 80% coverage on the in-domain test set.

          Known Limitations & Clinical Scope

          1. Dermatitis Sample Size: Only 93 training images (15 val, 15 test). The model merges Atopic Eczema and Contact Dermatitis due to overlapping spongiotic signs. F1 score is 0.62–0.71, so dermatitis predictions carry higher uncertainty.
          2. External SCIN Smartphone Domain Gap: Raw 4-class accuracy on the held-out SCIN benchmark (337 images, 82.5% macro close-ups, 72.7% Dermatitis, 0% Normal) is 20.77% (Macro-F1 0.1665), which rises to 44.51% Accuracy (Macro-F1 0.3321, Dermatitis recall 45.31%) under 3-class Symptomatic Triage Mode.
          3. Scope Constrained to 4 Classes: The model only recognizes Acne, Rosacea, Dermatitis, and Normal. It cannot identify skin cancers (melanoma, basal cell carcinoma), bacterial/fungal infections, or pigmentary disorders.
